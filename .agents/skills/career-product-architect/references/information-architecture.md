# Information architecture

Read project IA.md and PRODUCT.md as one freeze. Primary navigation: 市场探索器 → 职业探索 → 对比分析. Explorer views: 市场总览、地图视图、机会矩阵、行业结构. Display mode shares its data, filters and selection. Job list, local plans, favorites, methods, advanced six-metric analysis and trend limitations are explicit supporting tools.

Discovery is horizontal; career profiles are vertical; comparison starts from selected peers. Keep shared scope once above the work surface, preserve major→occupation synchronization, and present scope exclusions for comparisons. Matrix selection is local until explicit drill-down applies the exact cell filter. Maintain old links.
